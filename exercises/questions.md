# PromQL exercises (Module 2 · 4 minutes)

Open http://localhost:9090 → **Query** tab. Answer each question with one PromQL query.

1. **How busy?** How many requests per second is `/products` getting right now?
2. **How broken?** What fraction of `/checkout` calls are failing?
3. **What sells?** Which payment method was used most in the last 5 minutes?

Bonus: how many time series does `http_requests_total` have right now? Why that number?

Answers go up on the screen after 4 minutes. Try first, then ask your neighbour.
